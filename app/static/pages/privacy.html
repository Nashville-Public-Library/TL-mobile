Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Nashville Talking Library Mobile App and related audio services. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We collect limited usage data to help us improve our services and understand how our app is used.

Like most web services, our servers automatically collect information such as IP address, browser type, device type, and usage patterns. These logs are stored securely on our own servers and are not shared with third parties.

Data Usage

The data collected is used solely for internal analysis and service improvement. We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or other third parties.

Your Privacy

We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) unless you explicitly provide it. Any data collected is used in accordance with applicable privacy laws and best practices.

Contact Us
